WHAT IS AI AUTOMATION?

Multi-System Workflows, Not Just a Single Task

AI automation connects a trigger, an AI decision step, one or more tools or systems, and an outcome — often with a human approval step — into a single workflow. Instead of a person manually moving information between systems and making the same routine decisions repeatedly, the workflow runs the process end to end and brings a human in only where judgment or sign-off is needed.

Power Automate is a core tool for building these workflows, connecting AI models to email, forms, tickets, approvals and enterprise systems without hand-coding every integration.

Who's it for? Individuals who want to design and build these workflows themselves, and businesses that want the workflow built and operated for them without doing it in-house.

What's In a Workflow

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI automation?

AI automation connects a trigger, an AI decision step, one or more tools or systems, and an outcome — often including a human approval step — into a single workflow, so a multi-step business process runs automatically instead of requiring manual work at every stage.

Can I learn AI automation, or do I need it built for me?

Both are available. Individuals can learn to design and build these workflows themselves in Stream 2 of AI4Everyone's learning programs. Businesses that want the workflow built and operated for them can engage AI4Everyone's enterprise "Build With Us" service instead.

What tools are used for AI automation?

Power Automate is a core tool for multi-system workflow orchestration, alongside AI models, MCP-connected tools, and integrations with systems such as Jira, ServiceNow, Teams and SharePoint.

Does AI automation replace human oversight?

No. Well-designed AI automation workflows typically include a human approval step at key decision points, so AI handles the repetitive work while people retain oversight and final sign-off where it matters.
