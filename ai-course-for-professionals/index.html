Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take this AI course while working full-time?

Yes. Stream 1 — AI Builder tracks run 1 hour a day — 5 days for AI Foundations, 10 days for AI Creator, 7 days for AI Engineer — designed to fit around a full-time job rather than requiring a career break.

What will I be able to do after completing the professional track?

You progress from AI User to AI Creator to AI Engineer — starting with AI fundamentals and prompting, then building real web or mobile applications, then engineering AI-powered applications using APIs, agentic workflows and coding agents.

Do I need to already know how to code?

No traditional coding is required. AI does the coding — you learn to direct, test, verify and govern the engineering work AI produces. Existing coding knowledge is welcome and helps, but is not a prerequisite.

What if I want to go further into engineering AI systems for my company?

Developers, IT and cloud professionals can move from Stream 1 — AI Builder into Stream 2 — AI Engineering & Orchestration, which covers the AI technology stack, MCP and agent integration, and cloud/enterprise orchestration.
