Practical AI education and AI engineering — programs that teach you to use AI, build with AI, engineer AI systems, connect AI to tools, and orchestrate AI across cloud and enterprise systems.

Who is it for?

Students, professionals, developers, IT and cloud professionals, entrepreneurs, educators, and enterprises — anyone who wants to move from using AI to building and operating with it.

What will I be able to do?

Use AI → build applications → engineer AI systems → connect AI to tools → orchestrate cloud and enterprise workflows. Each step builds on the last.

THE DIFFERENCE

AI Does the Coding. You Engineer the Outcome.

This is not "learn AI without coding" in the simplistic sense. You still develop real technical understanding — APIs, models, architecture, databases, cloud, testing, security, integrations, automation, deployment, operations. You direct, test, verify and govern AI-generated engineering work instead of hand-writing every line yourself.

Traditional Approach

Learn programming → learn frameworks → write code → configure infrastructure → integrate systems → deploy → operate.

AI4Everyone Approach

Define the outcome → direct AI → AI generates/executes the required engineering work → connect tools → test → verify → deploy → operate.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI4Everyone?

AI4Everyone is a practical AI education and AI engineering platform. It teaches people to use AI, build applications with AI, engineer AI-powered systems, connect AI to tools, and orchestrate AI across cloud and enterprise systems. It is run by Keshav Karn, a 22+ year enterprise technology leader.

Is coding required?

No traditional coding is required. AI does the coding — you learn to direct, test, verify and govern the engineering work AI produces. Existing coding knowledge is welcome and helps, but is not a prerequisite.

What is Stream 1 — AI Builder?

Stream 1 — AI Builder takes you from AI User to AI Creator to AI Engineer across three tracks: AI Foundations, AI Creator, and AI Engineer. It's for anyone who wants to become AI-productive, build applications, and start an AI engineering journey.

What is Stream 2 — AI Engineering & Orchestration?

Stream 2 is the advanced program for developers, IT professionals, cloud engineers, DevOps/SRE, architects and automation professionals. It covers the AI technology stack, MCP and agent integration, and cloud/enterprise orchestration across three tracks: AI Engineering, AI Integration & Agent Engineering, and Cloud & Enterprise AI Orchestration.

What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) provides a standardized way for AI systems to access authorized tools and resources so AI can perform work instead of simply generating answers — for example, reading a Jira ticket, updating a ServiceNow record, or calling an internal API.

Who should learn AI engineering?

Developers, IT and cloud professionals, DevOps/SRE engineers, architects, automation professionals, and advanced AI builders who want to work with APIs, models, agents, MCP, and cloud/enterprise systems.

What can I build after AI Builder?

After Stream 1 — AI Builder, you can turn an idea into a working web or mobile application using AI-assisted development across GitHub, React, Node.js, databases, authentication and deployment.

What can I automate after AI Engineering & Orchestration?

You can connect AI to enterprise tools via MCP, build AI agents, provision and operate cloud infrastructure, perform cross-cloud migration, extract cloud data into Power BI dashboards, and automate multi-system enterprise workflows across Jira, ServiceNow, Teams and SharePoint.

Is there a free way to try AI4Everyone before enrolling?

Yes. AI4Everyone runs free introductory sessions with no cost and no commitment, where you can see AI-native engineering in action and ask questions directly.

Does AI4Everyone work with enterprises, not just individuals?

Yes. Separate from the individual learning programs, AI4Everyone offers enterprise AI services — custom AI tool development, cloud automation, ITSM/ITOM integration, and enterprise AI orchestration — delivered remotely worldwide.

Which cloud platforms and enterprise systems are covered?

AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud for cloud orchestration; Jira, ServiceNow, Power BI, Power Automate, Microsoft Teams and SharePoint for enterprise integration and automation.
