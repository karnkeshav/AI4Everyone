Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AI course for students cover?

The Stream 1 — AI Foundations track covers AI fundamentals, tokens, prompting and context, hallucinations, AI hygiene and privacy, NotebookLM and AI knowledge tools, RAG basics, and responsible AI — over 5 days at 1 hour per day.

Does AI4Everyone run workshops for schools and colleges?

Yes. Keshav conducts dedicated workshops for students and educators covering what to do with AI, what not to do, and how to use it responsibly to supercharge learning and careers.

Do students need coding experience to join?

No. No traditional coding is required for the AI Foundations track. Students who want to go further can progress into the AI Creator track to build real applications.

Is this only for school and college students?

The AI Foundations track and workshops are designed for students and educators, but the same track also works for early-career professionals building foundational AI literacy.
