Frequently Asked Questions

What is "Power BI AI"?

It's the use of AI coding agents to extract data from cloud and business systems, transform it, and build live Power BI dashboards — following the same Cloud/Data → Extraction → Transformation → Power BI → Dashboard flow taught in Stream 2's Cloud & Enterprise AI Orchestration track.

Can AI4Everyone build a Power BI dashboard for my business?

Yes — this is offered as an enterprise engagement, scoped individually and quoted after a consultation. See the enterprise AI services page for details.

Can I learn to build these dashboards myself instead?

Yes. Building Power BI dashboards with AI-directed data extraction and transformation is part of Track 3 (Cloud & Enterprise AI Orchestration) inside Stream 2 — AI Engineering & Orchestration.

Is pricing published for the dashboard-build service?

No. Like all enterprise engagements, Power BI dashboard builds are scoped individually and quoted in USD, GBP, EUR, or your local currency after an initial consultation.
